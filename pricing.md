# WealthBoost Pricing and Access

WealthBoost is free to download from the App Store.

New users can currently explore the full app for three days without starting an App Store subscription. After the introductory access period ends, an active subscription is required to continue.

The app currently presents one-month and twelve-month subscription options. Eligible users may see an introductory offer for the annual option. Prices, currencies, eligibility, renewal dates, and offer terms are displayed and managed by the App Store.

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled from App Store settings. Cancelling a subscription does not immediately remove access; access normally continues until the current entitlement expires.

The latest terms shown by the App Store at purchase time control the transaction.
