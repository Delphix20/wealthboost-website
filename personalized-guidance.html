PERSONALIZED GUIDANCE

Built around the goal you describe.

WealthBoost uses the details that affect your situation, so the advice for a mobile app does not read like the advice for a repair shop or restaurant.

Your goalYour audienceYour resourcesYour currency

Specific input gives the app something real to work with.

The profile can include what you are working on, what you are doing now, who the goal should help, the result you want, and the resources available to you.

Current situationYour stage changes which question or next step makes sense first.
Time and budgetAvailable resources help avoid suggestions that are unrealistic for the moment.
Local or onlineThe setting changes how users, customers, distribution, and testing should be considered.

Relevance is checked, not assumed.

WealthBoost can ask for a better description when profile information is vague, random, contradictory, or unrelated to the subject. The purpose is to avoid building a profile around text that does not express a usable direction.

When the profile changes, the app can request new guidance so Home, Plan, Ideas, and the AI Coach use the updated direction.

The user remains in control.

You decide what to share and can update the profile when your direction changes. WealthBoost does not require access to a bank account and does not need your entire financial life to prepare guidance.

Do not enter passwords, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, medical records, or other highly sensitive information into profile fields, notes, support messages, or Coach questions.
