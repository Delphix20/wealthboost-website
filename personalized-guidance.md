# Personalized Guidance in WealthBoost

WealthBoost builds guidance around the user's stated goal instead of selecting advice from a broad category alone.

Relevant profile details can include the activity, current situation, intended audience, desired result, available time, budget range, location context, country, and currency. Users can update these details when their direction changes.

## How relevance improves

- Specific goals produce more specific guidance.
- The intended audience helps WealthBoost avoid unrelated customer advice.
- Time and budget information keeps suggestions closer to what the user can realistically test.
- Local, online, or mixed context changes which ideas are appropriate.
- Feedback about usefulness helps identify directions that do or do not fit.

WealthBoost may ask the user to improve profile information when an answer is too vague, random, contradictory, or unrelated to the intended subject.
