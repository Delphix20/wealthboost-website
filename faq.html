FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about WealthBoost.

Direct answers about the goals you can use, personalization, subscriptions, privacy, and what the app is designed to do.

What kind of goal can I use?

You can use WealthBoost for a legitimate business, project, product, income direction, or practical change. Specific goals produce more useful guidance.

How is the guidance personalized?

WealthBoost uses the goal, audience, desired result, current situation, available time, budget range, location context, country, and currency supplied by the user. Feedback and saved guidance can also help the experience remain relevant.

Do I have to use WealthBoost every day?

No. Open WealthBoost whenever you need a new angle, useful idea, next step, or help with a decision.

What can I try before subscribing?

New users can currently explore the full app for three days. After that, an active subscription is required to continue. Available plans, introductory offers, eligibility, and local prices are shown by the App Store.

Which subscriptions are offered?

The current app presents one-month and twelve-month options. Existing three-month subscriptions remain valid until their entitlement ends but are no longer offered for new purchases.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancelling normally stops future renewal. Access remains available until the active App Store entitlement expires.

Is WealthBoost financial advice?

No. WealthBoost provides educational guidance and planning support. Important financial, legal, tax, health, or investment decisions should be reviewed with a qualified professional.

Does WealthBoost need access to my bank account?

No. WealthBoost does not require bank-account access and does not process financial transactions.

Can I use WealthBoost on iPad?

Yes. WealthBoost is designed for iPhone and iPad.

Can I save useful guidance?

Yes. Ideas, next steps, and AI Coach answers can be saved. Users can also create a personal note and mark saved guidance as used.
