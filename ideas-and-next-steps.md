# Ideas and Next Steps

WealthBoost presents ideas and next steps as different kinds of guidance.

An idea offers another direction to consider. It may improve, simplify, test, or rethink part of the user's goal.

A next step is a practical action or investigation that can help the user make progress or learn something useful. It is not intended to be an artificial completion button or a rigid daily mission.

Users can expand ideas, move between alternatives, save useful guidance, and provide feedback. The guidance should remain connected to the current goal and profile.
