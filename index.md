# WealthBoost

WealthBoost is an AI goal coach for iPhone and iPad. Tell WealthBoost what you want to create, change, or improve, and it prepares perspectives, ideas, next steps, and AI Coach answers around that goal.

## What WealthBoost provides

- A perspective that highlights something worth considering.
- A practical next move connected to the user's situation.
- Multiple goal-specific ideas and alternative directions.
- An AI Coach that already knows the current goal and profile.
- Saved guidance for ideas, next steps, Coach answers, and personal notes.
- Optional levels, streaks, and milestones that reward useful interactions.

WealthBoost does not require daily use and does not force a rigid long-term schedule. Open it whenever a new angle, useful idea, next step, or answer is needed.
