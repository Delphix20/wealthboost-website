SAVED GUIDANCE

Keep what is worth returning to.

Save selected ideas, next steps, and AI Coach answers in one place, then add your own note when the useful part came from you.

IdeasNext stepsCoach answersPersonal notes

Saving is intentional.

Guidance is added only when the user chooses to save it. Coach answers can be revealed with a left swipe, while ideas and next steps use their bookmark control.

Save an ideaKeep a direction that may be useful when the timing is right.
Save an answerReturn to a Coach explanation without searching the conversation again.
Write a noteKeep an observation, thought, or decision in your own words.

Use saved guidance in the real world.

A saved item can be marked as used after it helps with an actual decision, conversation, test, or change. Meaningful saved actions can contribute to progress systems such as XP and milestones.

Removing a saved item requires confirmation. The confirmation does not promise that every generated item can be recovered later.

Saved guidance is not a forced task list.

The purpose is to preserve information that matters, not to create another backlog that the user must complete. Items can remain available until they become relevant or are removed.
