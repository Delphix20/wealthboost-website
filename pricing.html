PRICING AND ACCESS

Explore first. Subscribe to continue.

WealthBoost is free to download. New users can currently explore the complete app for three days before an active subscription is required.

3-day app access1 month12 monthsApp Store billing

Introductory access begins after onboarding.

The app-controlled free access period currently lasts three days and does not require the user to start an App Store subscription. During that period, the full app is available, with a lower AI Coach usage limit for cost protection.

A countdown card on Home shows the remaining access and opens the subscription page. A stronger notice appears on the final day.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple.

One monthA recurring monthly option displayed in the user's App Store currency.
Twelve monthsAn annual option that may include an introductory offer for eligible users.
Existing three-month plansPreviously purchased three-month subscriptions remain valid until their entitlement ends but are no longer offered for new purchases.

Prices, currencies, introductory-offer eligibility, renewal dates, and transaction terms are supplied by the App Store. The information shown at purchase time controls the transaction.

Purchasing and cancelling behave differently.

When a user purchases during the app-controlled introductory period, StoreKit entitlement status replaces the three-day countdown. If an eligible annual purchase includes an App Store introductory offer, the offer follows Apple's terms.

Cancelling stops future renewal but normally leaves the app unlocked until the current entitlement expires. Subscriptions can be managed from App Store settings.
