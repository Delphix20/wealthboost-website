IDEAS AND NEXT STEPS

Another direction, or something useful to do next.

WealthBoost separates ideas from practical next steps so the user can tell whether the app is offering a possibility to consider or an action to take.

Explore alternativesExpand detailsSave guidanceRate relevance

An idea changes the angle.

An idea can help improve, simplify, test, or rethink part of the goal. It may identify another audience, offer, distribution method, validation approach, or way to reduce risk.

Ideas remain connected to the user's profile. A local business should not automatically receive an unrelated online-course suggestion simply because online products are common.

Improve the directionStrengthen something already connected to the current goal.
Test an assumptionFind out whether a belief about demand, users, pricing, or execution is true.
Consider an alternativeSee another legitimate route without abandoning the goal.

A next step produces information or movement.

A next step is a practical action or investigation. It should help the user make progress, answer an important question, or reduce uncertainty. It is not an artificial completion button and it does not require daily use.

The user can ask for another step, report an outcome when useful, or save the step for later.

Feedback helps reject the wrong direction.

Ideas can be rated as useful, partly useful, or not relevant. This feedback is sent to the WealthBoost backend and can help later guidance avoid repeating an unsuitable direction.

Useful ideas can be saved and revisited from Saved Guidance.
