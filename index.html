QUESTIONS

Before you download.

What kind of goal can I use?

You can use WealthBoost for a legitimate business, project, product, income direction, or practical change you want to make. Specific goals produce more useful guidance.

How is the guidance personalized?

WealthBoost uses the goal, audience, desired result, current situation, time, budget, and direction you provide. Your feedback and saved guidance also help the experience stay relevant.

Do I have to use it every day?

No. Open WealthBoost when you need a new angle, a next step, an idea, or help with a decision. Streaks and levels can reward useful interactions, but they do not control your goal.

What can I try before subscribing?

New users can explore the full app for three days. After that, a subscription is required to continue. Available plans, introductory offers, and local prices are shown in the App Store.

Is WealthBoost financial advice?

No. WealthBoost provides educational guidance and planning support. Important financial, legal, tax, or investment decisions should be reviewed with a qualified professional.

Can I use WealthBoost on iPad?

Yes. WealthBoost is designed for iPhone and iPad.
