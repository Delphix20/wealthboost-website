PRODUCT OVERVIEW

What is WealthBoost?

WealthBoost is an AI goal coach for people working on a business, project, product, income direction, or practical change.

iPhoneiPadGoal-specific guidanceAI Coach

Your goal becomes the context.

Tell WealthBoost what you want to create, change, or improve. The app uses that goal and the profile details you choose to provide to prepare perspectives, ideas, priorities, next steps, and AI Coach answers.

The purpose is not to give every user the same checklist. A restaurant, mobile app, repair shop, freelance service, and online store should produce different questions and different guidance.

Think differentlySee a relevant angle or assumption that may deserve attention.
Find a next stepGet a practical action or investigation connected to the current situation.
Ask in contextUse an AI Coach that already knows the current goal and profile.

Use it when direction matters.

WealthBoost does not require daily use and does not force a rigid long-term schedule. Open it whenever you need another angle, a useful idea, a next step, or help thinking through a decision.

  • Explore more than one way forward.
  • Organize what matters now, next, and later.
  • Save guidance that is worth returning to.
  • Add personal notes and mark saved guidance as used.

Know the boundaries.

WealthBoost is not a bank, budgeting service, investment platform, or financial institution. It does not require access to a bank account and it does not process financial transactions.

It provides educational guidance and planning support. AI-generated content can be incomplete or incorrect, and important financial, legal, tax, health, or investment decisions should be reviewed with a qualified professional.
