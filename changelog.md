# WealthBoost Product Updates

## Version 3.0

WealthBoost 3.0 is a complete product and interface rebuild centered on one principle: the user's goal should shape the entire experience.

Major changes include:

- A redesigned goal-focused Home page.
- Personalized perspectives and practical next steps.
- A simpler Plan that organizes what matters now, next, and later.
- Goal-specific ideas with expandable detail and feedback.
- An AI Coach connected to the user's current profile.
- Saved ideas, next steps, Coach answers, and personal notes.
- Updated levels, streaks, and meaningful milestones.
- A redesigned onboarding and profile flow.
- Updated iPhone, iPad, Home Screen, and Lock Screen experiences.

The official App Store version and release notes remain the source for current availability.
