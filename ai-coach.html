AI COACH

Ask about the decision in front of you.

The Coach uses your current goal and relevant profile details, so you can ask a direct question without explaining the entire situation again.

Contextual answersSuggested questionsSave useful answers

Use the Coach to think through something specific.

Ask about a decision, obstacle, idea, offer, customer or audience, next step, or another subject connected to the goal you described.

A decisionCompare options, tradeoffs, timing, or what information is still missing.
An obstacleBreak down what may be causing the problem and what can be tested next.
An ideaImprove, simplify, question, or expand an idea without losing the goal context.

Answers are formatted for the question.

The Coach can use normal paragraphs, short headings, and lists when lists are genuinely helpful. It does not force every answer into the same template.

Swipe on a Coach answer to reveal the save control. Saved answers remain available in Saved Guidance together with ideas, next steps, and personal notes.

AI output still requires judgment.

AI-generated information can be incomplete or incorrect. Verify important facts and do not treat a Coach answer as professional financial, legal, tax, health, or investment advice.

Do not include passwords, payment-card numbers, government identifiers, medical records, or other highly sensitive information in Coach questions.
