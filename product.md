# What WealthBoost Is

WealthBoost is an AI goal coach for people working on a business, project, product, income direction, or practical change.

The app uses a goal and the supporting profile details supplied by the user to prepare tailored perspectives, useful ideas, practical next steps, priorities, and AI Coach answers.

## Intended use

WealthBoost is useful when someone wants to:

- Think through an idea from another angle.
- Decide what deserves attention first.
- Find a practical next step.
- Explore alternatives connected to the same goal.
- Ask a contextual AI Coach about a decision or obstacle.
- Save useful guidance and return to it later.

## What WealthBoost is not

WealthBoost is not a bank, budgeting service, investment platform, or financial institution. It does not require access to a bank account and does not process transactions.

It provides educational guidance and planning support. Important financial, legal, tax, health, or investment decisions should be reviewed with a qualified professional.
